# More tab (decided with Shawn, 2026-10-17)

The Settings tab became **More** (ellipsis icon): a short list of rows that
each open their own screen, so the tab has room for things that aren't
settings, such as contacts.

- Tabs: **Home · Activity · More · Search**.
- More lists **Settings** (appearance, message style, themes, app icon,
  experimental), **Notifications**, **Accounts** (each signed-in account;
  the plus in the header adds one), and **About** (version, channel,
  update) on its own below them.
- Routes live under `/more`: `/more/settings`, `/more/notifications`,
  `/more/accounts`, `/more/account`, `/more/about`.
