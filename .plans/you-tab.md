# You tab (decided with Shawn, 2026-10-17)

The Settings tab became **You** (person icon): a short list of rows that
each open their own screen, so the tab has room for things that aren't
settings, such as contacts. "More" with an ellipsis was tried first and
read as overflow; "You" names what's there (as YouTube's tab does).

- Tabs: **Home · Activity · You · Search**.
- The You page has no title: the tab bar already names it.
- It lists **Settings** (appearance, message style, themes, app icon,
  experimental), **Notifications**, **Accounts** (each signed-in account;
  the plus in the header adds one), and **About** (version, channel,
  update) on its own below them.
- Routes live under `/you`: `/you/settings`, `/you/notifications`,
  `/you/accounts`, `/you/account`, `/you/about`.
